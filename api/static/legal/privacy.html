Whot Privacy Policy

Last updated September 4, 2026

Overview

Whot (“the App”) is an online multiplayer card game. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We create a random guest player ID stored on your device so you can reconnect to games. If you set a display name, we store it on your device and send it to our game server while you play. We also process gameplay data (moves, lobby membership, reconnect state) needed to run matches. We do not require an account, email, or phone number to play.

How we use information

Guest IDs and display names identify you at the table and support reconnect. Gameplay data runs the match in real time. Local preferences (theme, sound mute) stay on your device.

Where data is processed

Gameplay traffic is sent over HTTPS/WSS to our backend so matches can be hosted online. The App needs a network connection to create, join, and play tables.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Other players at your table may see your display name and public game state. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the App.

Retention

Guest IDs and display names remain on your device until you clear app data or reinstall. Active game sessions are kept on the server only as long as needed to run or expire the table.

Children

The App is a general-audience card game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is required for guest play.
